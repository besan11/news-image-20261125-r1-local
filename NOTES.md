## Dating traps avoided

- Huawei verdict: convicted Tue 24 Nov (DOJ release + Reuters, both dated
  24 Nov). Huawei's own statement came 25 Nov, which is why the story
  looked like a Wednesday headline. It is in the DATE CHECK, not a lead.
- UN Femicide Brief (47,000 women killed by partners/family in 2025):
  published Tue 24 Nov, ahead of the 25 Nov observance day. DATE CHECK.
- Leaked Russian war deaths ~490,000, CAS restoring Senegal's AFCON title,
  and the Gaza strike killing a Hamas security chief were all first
  reported 24 Nov. DATE CHECK.
- COP31 collapse (Sun 22 Nov) also excluded. Spain votes Sun 30 Nov —
  listed as not yet.
